# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript (explicitly requested).

## Users

People in Colombia who want to buy a refreshing frozen fruit-and-yogurt treat for their dog.

## Product Purpose

COOL ANIMAL presents and sells fruit popsicles for dogs, helping customers discover the flavors and place a simple delivery order.

## Positioning

Frozen popsicles made with natural yogurt and fruit, offered as a refreshing moment to share with a dog.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Strawberry and banana flavors; listed price is COP 3,500 per popsicle.
- The storefront needs a working cart, checkout summary, configurable delivery fee and WhatsApp order message.
- WhatsApp phone, Instagram username, delivery fee and payment methods are configured in `config.js`.
- Do not invent business contact details, payment account numbers, or social links.
- Mobile-first, static site with no frameworks.

## Brand Commitments

COOL ANIMAL. “Refresca. Consiente. Comparte.” Freshness, care, naturalness, fun, trust, and affection for pets. Corporate palette from the supplied project: cocoa `#4B2D19`, antique cream `#F1E9DB`, tiger orange `#FF8800`, golden yellow `#FFD700`, soft mint `#C7EED4`, and glacier blue `#A8DADC`. Use orange and gold for primary emphasis, cream as the base, and the supplied logo artwork without recoloring. Voice is brief, friendly, and commercial.

## Evidence on Hand

The supplied brief contains the product copy and price. The project document supplies official logo artwork and the six-color identity. No official product photographs, social account, WhatsApp number, or delivery price were supplied. Other visuals are replaceable local placeholders.

## Product Principles

- Explain the product and price quickly.
- Keep product imagery and ordering prominent.
- Make the purchase flow short and usable on phones.
- Keep configurable business details out of invented copy.
